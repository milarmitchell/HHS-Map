Academic Conversation Skills — Posters & Notebook Reference

Five wall posters (one per skill from Zwiers & Crawford), each with two sentence frames per WIDA level 1–6. Followed by three notebook reference sheets — condensed models students can glue in and use while building their own chart.

Print with Cmd/Ctrl + P. Page size and breaks are already set for Letter, landscape — one poster or reference sheet per page.

Wall poster 1 of 5

Elaborating and Clarifying

Explaining an idea in more detail, or asking a partner to explain further.

Skill 1 of 5 · WIDA levels 1–6
Level 1
Entering
"More about ___." (with pointing/visual)
"I mean ___." (with pointing/visual)
Level 2
Emerging
"I mean that ___."
"Can you say more?"
Level 3
Developing
"What I mean is ___ because ___."
"Can you explain what you mean by ___?"
Level 4
Expanding
"To clarify, I'm saying that ___, specifically because ___."
"When you say ___, do you mean ___?"
Level 5
Bridging
"Let me elaborate on that — ___."
"I want to make sure I understand: are you suggesting that ___?"
Level 6
Reaching
"In other words, ___, which matters because ___."
"Let me unpack that a bit further: ___."

Supporting Ideas with Evidence

Backing up a claim with a reason, example, or textual detail.

Skill 2 of 5 · WIDA levels 1–6
Level 1
Entering
"Because ___." (pointing to text/image)
"Look — ___." (pointing to evidence)
Level 2
Emerging
"I think ___ because ___."
"The picture/text shows ___."
Level 3
Developing
"My evidence is ___. This shows ___."
"I know this because ___."
Level 4
Expanding
"One piece of evidence that supports my idea is ___, which shows that ___."
"This detail proves my point because ___."
Level 5
Bridging
"The text states that ___, which supports my claim because ___."
"Based on the evidence, it's clear that ___."
Level 6
Reaching
"This is supported by ___, as evidenced by ___."
"The strongest evidence for this is ___, since it directly demonstrates ___."

Building On and Challenging Ideas

Agreeing, disagreeing, or adding to a partner's idea respectfully.

Skill 3 of 5 · WIDA levels 1–6
Level 1

"I disagree." (thumbs down)
Level 2
Emerging
"I agree with ___ because ___."
"I disagree because ___."
Level 3
Developing
"I want to add to what ___ said: ___."
"I see it differently because ___."
Level 4
Expanding
"Building on what ___ said, I think ___."
"I understand your point, but I think ___ because ___."
Level 5
Bridging
"That's an interesting point — I'd like to add ___."
"I see your point, but have you considered ___?"
Level 6
Reaching
"While ___ raises a valid point, I'd argue ___ because ___."
"That builds nicely on ___; I'd push it further by saying ___."

Building your own notebook chart

  1. Fold or draw a 3-column table across two facing pages: WIDA Level, Frame A, Frame B.
  2. Copy the six level names down the left side: Entering, Emerging, Developing, Expanding, Bridging, Reaching.
  3. Using the class poster as your model, write two sentence frames for your own current level, then one level up as a stretch goal.
  4. Leave the other rows for frames you hear classmates use during discussion — add to the chart all year.
  5. Star the frame you'll try to use in your very next conversation.
